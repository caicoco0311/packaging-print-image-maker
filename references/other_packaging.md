# Other-packaging positive prompt blocks

## common

```text
Lock the functional silhouette, capacity cues, opening or dispensing mechanism, material, seams, and printable areas for the requested bottle, tube, pouch, bag, can, jar, sleeve, wrap, or other package.
```

## role_2

```text
Show 3–6 complete large packages in the clearest structure-specific production or assortment view.
```

## role_4

```text
Show the packaging holding, dispensing, protecting, transporting, or presenting the corresponding product plausibly.
```

## role_5

```text
Show complete large examples of the principal print zone or surface decoration at readable scale.
```

## role_6

```text
Include at least three views that reveal the most important functional structure without changing identity.
```
